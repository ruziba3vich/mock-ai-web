Welcome to the Elm Street community garden. New members can collect their gate keys from the library desk, not from the garden shed. The library is open until six on weekdays. Our shared work session takes place on the first Sunday of each month, starting at nine in the morning. You may have seen an old notice mentioning Saturday; please ignore that. Bring gardening gloves. Tools are supplied and must be returned to the shed after use. Water is collected in two large tanks behind the greenhouse. During dry weather, please use watering cans rather than the hose. The annual membership fee is twenty-four pounds. Students pay half that amount if they show a valid student card. Finally, please leave the narrow path beside the fence clear because it is our emergency access route.
