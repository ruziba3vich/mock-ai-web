Thank you for calling the Riverside Museum. Our Saturday photography workshop will take place on the fourteenth of November. The morning session was originally advertised for ten o'clock, but it will now start at half past ten because the main gallery opens later that day. Please arrive fifteen minutes before the session begins. The workshop costs eighteen pounds per person, including a printed guide. You do not need a professional camera: a phone camera is fine. Please bring a notebook, however, as we cannot supply one. Meet the tutor at the east entrance, opposite the bicycle racks. Do not wait by the gift shop. The workshop is limited to twelve people, so booking is essential. To reserve a place, give your name and contact number to the reception team.
